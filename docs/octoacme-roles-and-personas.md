# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers lead user research, design, and testing activities. They ensure that products are intuitive, accessible, and delightful. They collaborate closely with Product Managers to understand customer needs and with Developers to ensure design intent is faithfully implemented.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Advocate for user needs and accessibility standards
- Collaborate with Product Managers on requirements and user stories
- Review implementations to ensure fidelity to design specifications
- Document design decisions and maintain design systems

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered, accessible products
- Reduce friction and improve user satisfaction
- Ensure consistency across product experiences
- Minimize design-to-implementation gaps

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and usability testing sessions
- Collaboration on feature requirements and acceptance criteria
- Design documentation and style guides
- Cross-functional alignment on user experience priorities

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers own user-facing and internal documentation. They ensure that all deliverables—user guides, API documentation, release notes, and process documentation—are clear, accurate, and accessible. They collaborate with Developers and Product Managers to capture features and with Support teams to address user questions.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user guides, API documentation, and tutorials
- Draft release notes and communicate feature changes
- Document internal processes and operational procedures
- Review deliverables for clarity and completeness
- Incorporate user feedback and support tickets into documentation
- Ensure documentation aligns with delivered features

### Goals
- Enable users and team members to self-serve with clear documentation
- Reduce support burden through comprehensive, searchable docs
- Maintain documentation currency and accuracy
- Support product adoption through clear communication

### Typical Communication
- Collaboration with Developers during feature design and delivery
- Sync with Product Managers on feature prioritization and messaging
- Feedback from Support and QA teams on common questions
- Documentation reviews and style guide maintenance

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning, execution, and communication of releases. They ensure that all pre-release requirements are met, oversee deployment processes, and communicate schedules and status to stakeholders. They work closely with QA, DevOps, and engineering teams to ensure smooth, low-risk releases.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release schedules and coordinate with all stakeholders
- Manage pre-release checklists and acceptance criteria verification
- Coordinate with DevOps and engineering on deployment activities
- Document and communicate rollback plans and mitigation strategies
- Manage release notes and communicate changes to internal teams and customers
- Monitor post-deployment metrics and trigger incident response if needed
- Maintain release calendars and track deployment history

### Goals
- Execute reliable, predictable releases with minimal risk
- Reduce time-to-market while maintaining quality
- Ensure clear communication and transparency throughout the release cycle
- Enable rapid incident response and rollback when necessary

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and coordination meetings
- Deployment status updates and stakeholder notifications
- Post-deployment verification and metrics reviews
- Incident response and escalation channels

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads manage customer and internal support activities, gather user feedback, and serve as a bridge between customers and the product team. They triage issues, identify patterns, and escalate systemic problems to Product and Project Managers for continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Manage support ticket triage and prioritization
- Gather and synthesize user feedback and pain points
- Identify patterns in support requests and escalate to product team
- Collaborate with QA on issue verification and reproduction
- Provide input on product roadmap based on customer needs
- Create and maintain support documentation and FAQs
- Monitor customer satisfaction and product quality metrics

### Goals
- Deliver excellent customer support and fast issue resolution
- Reduce support volume through product improvements and documentation
- Provide early signals on product issues and customer needs
- Build strong customer relationships and gather market intelligence

### Typical Communication
- Daily issue triage and escalation meetings
- Weekly sync with Product and Project Managers on common issues
- Feedback sessions with QA and engineering teams
- Customer satisfaction reviews and retrospectives
